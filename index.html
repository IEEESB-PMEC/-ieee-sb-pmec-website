Welcome to IEEE STB PMEC

Cultivating the Future of Technology & Engineering

Established in 2026 under the IEEE Bhubaneswar Section, our Student Branch at Parala Maharaja Engineering College (PMEC) is a gateway for students into the global IEEE community driving innovation across electronics, computing, and emerging technologies.

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization, advancing technology for the benefit of humanity across 160+ countries. At PMEC, our Student Branch empowers students through workshops, hackathons, research projects, and industry exposure building leadership, innovation, and technical excellence across every engineering discipline.

Core Areas of Focus:
Electronics & VLSI Embedded & IoT AI & Emerging Tech Research & Leadership

Vision Statement

Promoting excellence and innovation across all fields of engineering and technology for the benefit of humanity, in line with IEEE's global mission.

Mission Statement

To foster the professional growth of our members through access to research, technical knowledge, and industry exposure building leadership, teamwork, and innovation through workshops, competitions, and collaborative projects.

Field of Interest

Spans a wide range of engineering and technology domains including electronics, VLSI, embedded systems, IoT, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies delivering research, innovation, and industry-ready skills across every branch of engineering.

Inauguration

Branch Launch

The official inaugural ceremony of the branch, followed by IEEE Day 2025 celebrations and the branch's first IEEE webinar marking the beginning of our journey at PMEC Berhampur.

First 'Made in India' Chips

National Milestone

PMEC Berhampur developed the High Performance Multiplier IC-C2S0061, suitable for hardware-based face recognition (criminal tracking) leveraging FPGA and ASIC technologies. This represents a monumental step forward in India's semiconductor self-reliance mission under #MakeInIndia and #AatmanirbharBharat.

AIMLCPS 2026

International Conference

1st International Conference on "Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning in Communication and Power Systems" (AIMLCPS 2026), held on 20 21 February 2026. Jointly organized by the Department of Electrical Engineering & Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at Parala Maharaja Engineering College, Berhampur.

C2S Program at PMEC

Institutional Milestone

The Chips-to-Startup (C2S) program execution details, featuring the official program approval letter, the research design team at PMEC, and the custom testboards integrating the ASIC silicon die.

Associated & Affiliated Organizations

We work in collaboration with key technical councils, departments, and societies to foster excellence in electronic design and engineering.

IEEE

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE EDS PMEC

The IEEE Electron Devices Society Student Branch at PMEC, driving hands-on learning in semiconductor physics, VLSI and PCB design, and IoT prototyping through workshops, guest lectures, hackathons, and research projects.

IEEE Bhubaneswar Section

Our local IEEE section overseeing Odisha, providing technical sponsorship, funding support, and steering the overall professional growth of IEEE activities in the region.

PMEC Berhampur

Parala Maharaja Engineering College is our host institution and a premier government engineering college of Odisha, providing resources, state-of-the-art labs, and academic mentorship.

IEEE CEDA

The IEEE Council on Electronic Design Automation bridges academia, industry, and government. CEDA's field of interest spans the theory, implementation, and use of EDA/CAD tools to design integrated electronic systems.

ETIOS Society

The official technical society of the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at PMEC, focused on hosting seminars, expert talks, and technical department activities to encourage innovation.
